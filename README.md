# Mongolian AI Dubbing

**Watch:** https://nasanochir.github.io/mongolian-ai-dubbing/

Full episodes dubbed into Mongolian by a voice model I trained on a ~92-hour, 95-speaker Mongolian speech corpus.

- `media/indulge_ep1_mn_dub.mp4`: live-action drama, episode 1 (4:52), multi-character, emotional dialogue
- `media/naruto_pain_mn_dub.mp4`: anime out-of-domain test (8:39), shouting and battle scenes

Clips are non-commercial technology demonstrations. Original video and characters belong to their respective rights holders; contact me for removal.
