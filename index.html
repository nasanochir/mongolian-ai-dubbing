Mongolian AI Dubbing

Full episodes dubbed into Mongolian by a voice model I trained on a ~92-hour, 95-speaker Mongolian speech corpus. Every voice you hear is generated by the model. Each character keeps one consistent voice, and every line passes automated checks for words, voice, pitch and timing before it ships. Subtitles are automated too: the source subtitles are read (including text burned into the video), rewritten as natural Mongolian fitted to each line's timing, and burned into the episode.

Live-action drama: Episode 1 (4:52)

Many characters, male and female leads, emotional dialogue (sad, tense, angry).

Anime: Naruto Shippuden, Pain arc excerpt (8:39)

Out-of-domain test: anime voice acting, battle shouting, creatures and a large cast.
